About theFAUND How it works

We work with one material — hyaluronic acid — and two proprietary ways of cross-linking it. DéMORO's Planetary Motion Cross-Linking and BiViDerma's Multi Layered Crosslinking each tune the gel for the depth and area it is meant to treat, so practitioners can choose with precision.

Why hyaluronic acid

Three things give skin its youth. One of them holds water.

Hyaluronic acid binds more water than any other natural or synthetic polymer — a single gram can hold up to six litres. Injected as a cross-linked gel, it restores volume, softens wrinkles and supports facial contours with a natural finish.
